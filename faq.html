Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Checkout accepts PayPal and major credit/debit cards through PayPal's secure system. Cash on delivery may be available in some areas.

Why do you ask for my location?

We need an exact delivery address before payment so your parcel goes to the right place. You can type it or use "Use my current location" to fill it automatically.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 7–21 days depending on destination. You'll get a tracking number once shipped.

Can I cancel or return an order?

Contact us before it ships to cancel. Unused items can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

Is my card information safe?

Yes. Card details are entered on PayPal's secure fields; we never see or store them.
