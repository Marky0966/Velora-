Shipping & returns

Shipping

We ship worldwide. Shipping is free above the threshold shown at checkout; otherwise a flat fee applies. Typical delivery is 7–21 business days. Customs duties and import taxes, if any, are the buyer's responsibility.

Returns

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Contact us to start a return. Refunds go back to your original payment method once the item is received.

Damaged or wrong item?

Send us a photo within 7 days and we'll replace or refund it.
